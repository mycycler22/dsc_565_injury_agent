Title: Epidemiology of acromioclavicular joint injury in young athletes. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 22707749 | Year: 2012 | Injury query: AC joint injury
[RESULTS] Acromioclavicular injuries resulted in at least 1359 total days lost to injury and an average of 18.4 days lost per athlete.
[CONCLUSION] The average time lost to sport due to AC joint injury was 18 days, with low-grade injuries averaging 10 days lost.
[CONCLUSION] High-grade injuries averaged 64 days lost to sport, and 71% elected to undergo surgical repair/reconstruction.
