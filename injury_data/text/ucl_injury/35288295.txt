Title: Return to play after ulnar collateral ligament reconstruction with concurrent posterior-compartment decompression. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 35288295 | Year: 2022 | Injury query: UCL injury
[RESULTS] Of the 12 patients, 10 returned to play at the same level (4 Major League Baseball players and 1 Triple A player) or at a higher level (2 players from Single A to Double A, 1 player from National Collegiate Athletic Association Division I to Single A, 1 player from rookie league to Single A, and 1 player from high school to National Collegiate Athletic Association Division I), 1 did not return to play, and 1 returned to play at a lower level, with an average RTP time of 14.64 months (standard deviation, 2.64 months).
