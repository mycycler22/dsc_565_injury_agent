[FULLTEXT: Results:] A total of 11 pitchers met inclusion criteria (45% professional and 55% collegiate) and had follow-up data collected (mean follow-up time, 2.9 years; range, 1.0-5.0 years). All 11 (100%) were able to return to sport at the preinjury level or higher, and the mean time from surgery to return to competition was 9 months. At follow-up, the mean function score of the American Shoulder and Elbow Surgeons elbow form was 35.8 (out of 36), the mean Kerlan-Jobe Orthopaedic Clinic score was 88.3 (out of 100), and the mean Andrews-Carson score was 95.0 (out of 100).
