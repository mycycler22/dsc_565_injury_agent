Title: Medial patellofemoral ligament reconstruction for recurrent patellar dislocation allows a good rate to return to sport. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 34846539 | Year: 2022 | Injury query: patellar dislocation
[RESULTS] The mean time from surgery to return to sports was 10.4 ± 8.6 months (range: 2-48 months). 62 patients (67%) returned to the same (50 patients) or higher (12 patients) level. 22 patients (26%) returned at a lower level. 19 of these patients attributed this decreased participation to ongoing knee problems.
[CONCLUSION] Mean time to return to sports was 10 months and post-operative Tegner score was noted to be modestly decreased from pre-injury level.
