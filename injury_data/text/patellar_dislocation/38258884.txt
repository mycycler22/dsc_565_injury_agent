Title: Consensus-Based Guidelines for Management of First-Time Patellar Dislocation in Adolescents. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 38258884 | Year: 2024 | Injury query: patellar dislocation
[RESULTS] Those that met the criteria for consensus included initial knee radiographs (99% consensus), nonoperative treatment for first-time patellar dislocation without an osteochondral fracture (99%), physical therapy starting within the first month postinjury (99%), with return to sport after 2 to 4 months (68%) with a brace (75%) and further follow-up as needed (75%).
