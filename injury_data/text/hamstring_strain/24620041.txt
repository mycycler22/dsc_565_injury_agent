Title: Acute hamstring injuries in Swedish elite sprinters and jumpers: a prospective randomised controlled clinical trial comparing two rehabilitation protocols. | Type: Comparative Study, Journal Article, Randomized Controlled Trial, Research Support, Non-U.S. Gov't | PMID: 24620041 | Year: 2014 | Injury query: hamstring strain
[RESULTS] Time to return was significantly shorter for the athletes in the L-protocol, mean 49 days (1SD±26, range 18-107 days), compared with the C-protocol, mean 86 days (1SD±34, range 26-140 days).
[RESULTS] Irrespective of protocol, hamstring injuries where the proximal free tendon was involved took a significantly longer time to return than injuries that did not involve the free tendon, L-protocol: mean 73 vs 31 days and C-protocol: mean 116 vs 63 days, respectively.
