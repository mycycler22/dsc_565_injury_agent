Title: A comparison of 2 rehabilitation programs in the treatment of acute hamstring strains. | Type: Clinical Trial, Comparative Study, Journal Article, Randomized Controlled Trial, Research Support, Non-U.S. Gov't | PMID: 15089024 | Year: 2004 | Injury query: hamstring strain
[RESULTS] The average (+/- SD) time required to return to sports for athletes in the STST group was 37.4 +/- 27.6 days, while the average time for athletes in the PATS group was 22.2 +/- 8.3 days.
[RESULTS] In the first 2 weeks after return to sports, reinjury rate was significantly greater (P = .00343, Fisher's exact test) in the STST group, where 6 of 11 athletes (54.5%) suffered a recurrent hamstring strain after completing the stretching and strengthening program, as compared to none of the 13 athletes (0%) in the PATS group.
[RESULTS] After 1 year of return to sports, reinjury rate was significantly greater (P = .0059, Fisher's exact test) in the STST group.
