Title: Clinical predictors of time to return to competition following hamstring injuries. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 25489558 | Year: 2014 | Injury query: hamstring strain
[RESULTS] by univariate analysis, clinical criteria associated with a full recovery time >40 days were VAS pain score greater than 6, popping sound injury, pain during everyday activities for more than 3 days, bruising, and greater than 15° motion-range limitation.
[RESULTS] By multivariate analysis, only VAS pain score and pain during everyday activities were significantly associated with time to recovery >40 days (53% sensitivity, 95% specificity).
