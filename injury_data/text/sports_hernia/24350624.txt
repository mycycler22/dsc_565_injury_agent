Title: Effect of pubic bone marrow edema on recovery from endoscopic surgery for athletic pubalgia. | Type: Journal Article, Randomized Controlled Trial | PMID: 24350624 | Year: 2015 | Injury query: sports hernia
[UNLABELED] The presence of pre-operative BME was graded from 0 to 3 using MRI and correlated to post-operative pain scores and recovery to sports activity 2 years after operation.
[UNLABELED] Periostic and intraosseous edema at symphysis pubis was related to increase of post-operative pain scores only at 3 months after surgery (P = 0.03) but not to long-term recovery.
