Title: The prevention of ankle sprains in sports. A systematic review of the literature. | Type: Journal Article, Systematic Review | PMID: 10569362 | Year: 1999 | Injury query: ankle sprain
[UNLABELED] Based on our review, we recommend that athletes with a sprained ankle complete supervised rehabilitation before returning to practice or competition, and those athletes suffering a moderate or severe sprain should wear an appropriate orthosis for at least 6 months.
