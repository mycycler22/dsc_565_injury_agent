Title: Isometric exercise during immobilization reduces the time to return to play after lateral ankle sprain. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 34547600 | Year: 2021 | Injury query: ankle sprain
[UNLABELED] The intervention reduced the side-to-side difference in total ROM (20° versus 31°, p = 0.01) and time to RTP (46 versus 65 days, p = 0.01) compared to the control group. [Conclusion] Increased deficiency in ankle ROM led to a longer time to RTP, and isometric exercise combined with EMS during immobilization increased the total ankle ROM and shortened the time to RTP.
