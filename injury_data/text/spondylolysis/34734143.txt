[FULLTEXT: Results] The cohort included 13 elite (7 state and 6 international) cricket fast bowlers from 3 countries (New Zealand, Australian and India) with an average age of 26 years (range, 20.3–29.5 years). All returned to play professional cricket at a median time of 8 months (IQR, 7–11 months) post surgery. All ten players surveyed at final follow-up [median, 38 (IQR, 31–103) months, range, 15–197 months] rated their bowling performance as the ‘same or better’ compared with prior to surgery. At final follow-up, 10 players continue to play cricket professionally ranging from 15 to 107 months post-surgery [median 35 (IQR, 24–43) months].
