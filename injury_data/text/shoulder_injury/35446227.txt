Title: Factors affecting the shoulder functional profile in elite judo athletes. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 35446227 | Year: 2023 | Injury query: shoulder injury
[UNLABELED] Sixty-one elite judo athletes (38 males, age: 18.1 ± 1.2 years, body mass: 69.3 ± 13.3 kg, body height: 172.2 ± 9.8 cm, brown belt to second-degree black belt) completed three questionnaires: Western Ontario Shoulder Instability, Western Ontario Rotator Cuff, and Shoulder Instability-Return to Sport after Injury.
