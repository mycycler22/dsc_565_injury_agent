Title: Editorial Commentary: Hip Arthroscopy Wins the National Basketball Association Slam Dunk Contest! | Type: Editorial, Comment | PMID: 32014178 | Year: 2020 | Injury query: femoroacetabular impingement
[UNLABELED] When considering hip arthroscopy to address a femoroacetabular impingement condition, 3 big questions are asked by every athlete and must be answered: (1) Can I play again? (2) Will I play at the same level? (3) When can I return to sport? Regarding the National Basketball Association, approximately 88% of athletes return to play at the same level in about 6 months.
