Title: Return to Sport in Athletes With Borderline Hip Dysplasia After Hip Arthroscopy for Femoroacetabular Impingement Syndrome. | Type: Journal Article, Research Support, Non-U.S. Gov't | PMID: 34825840 | Year: 2022 | Injury query: femoroacetabular impingement
[RESULTS] A total of 31 (75.6%) patients were able to RTS after hip arthroscopy at a mean of 8.3 ± 3.2 months.
[CONCLUSION] Of the patients with BHD studied here, 75.6% of patients successfully returned to sport at a mean of 8.3 ± 3.2 months after hip arthroscopy for FAIS.
