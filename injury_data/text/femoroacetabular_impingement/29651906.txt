Title: Return to Sport Rates in Physically Active Individuals 6 Months After Arthroscopy for Femoroacetabular Impingement Syndrome. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 29651906 | Year: 2019 | Injury query: femoroacetabular impingement
[CONCLUSIONS] Though just over half of participants returned to sport 6 months after hip arthroscopy for FAIS, only 18% returned to their previous level of sports participation.
