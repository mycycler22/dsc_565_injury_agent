Title: Surgical Management of Jones Fractures in Athletes: Orthobiologic Augmentation: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of 718 Fractures. | Type: Meta-Analysis, Systematic Review, Journal Article | PMID: 35612823 | Year: 2023 | Injury query: Jones fracture
[RESULTS] In the biologically augmented fixation group, successful RTP was reported in 195 (98.98%) of 197 fractures (odds ratio [OR], 97.5%; 95% CI, 95.8%-100%; <i>I</i><sup>2</sup> = 0), with a mean time to RTP of 10.3 weeks (95% CI, 9.5-11.1 weeks; <i>I</i><sup>2</sup> = 99%).
[RESULTS] In the group that received fixation without biological augmentation, successful RTP was reported in 516 (99.04%) of 521 fractures (OR, 98.7%; 95% CI, 97.8%-99.7%; <i>I</i><sup>2</sup> = 0], with a mean time to RTP of 9.7 weeks (95% CI, 7.84-11.53 weeks; <i>I</i><sup>2</sup> = 98.64%].
[RESULTS] In the biologically augmented fixation group, fracture union was achieved in 194 (98.48%) of 197 fractures (OR, 97.6%; 95% CI, 95.5%-99.7%; <i>I</i><sup>2</sup> = 0%), with a mean time to fracture union of 9.28 weeks (95% CI, 7.23-11.34 weeks; <i>I</i><sup>2</sup> = 98.18%).
[RESULTS] In the group that received fixation without biological augmentation, fracture union was achieved in 407 (93.78%) of 434 fractures (OR, 97.4%; 95% CI, 96%-98.9%; <i>I</i><sup>2</sup> = 0%), with a mean time to fracture union of 8.57 weeks (95% CI, 6.82-10.32 weeks; <i>I</i><sup>2</sup> = 98.81%).
