Title: Hip injuries and labral tears in the national football league. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 18641370 | Year: 2008 | Injury query: muscle strain
[RESULTS] There were a total of 23 806 injuries from 1997 to 2006, of which 738 were hip injuries (3.1%) with an average of 12.3 days lost per injury.
[CONCLUSION] A majority of these injuries are minor, with a return to play within 2 weeks.
