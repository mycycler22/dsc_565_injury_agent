[FULLTEXT: Results:] The sites of injuries were distributed as follows: hamstrings (n = 33), quadriceps (n = 6), hip adductors (n = 6), and calves (n = 10). MRI findings revealed 9 muscle strains (16%), 19 grade 1a (34%), 19 grade 1b (34%), and 8 grade 2 muscle strains (16%). The length of time until return to training and competition, respectively, was 15 and 26 days for grade 1a injuries, 19 and 36 days for grade 1b injuries, and 55 and 69 days for grade 2 injuries.
