Title: Accelerated Return to Competition Following a Full-Thickness Abdominal Wall Tear in an Elite Sprinter: A Data-informed Case Report. | Type: Case Reports, Journal Article | PMID: 42549193 | Year: 2026 | Injury query: muscle strain
[UNLABELED] Progression was guided by athlete response and objective performance data rather than predetermined time-based criteria. # Outcomes Pain-free sprinting was achieved within 16 days, unrestricted training resumed by Day 18, and the athlete returned to competition on Day 28, recording a lifetime personal best of 10.08 seconds in the 100 m.
