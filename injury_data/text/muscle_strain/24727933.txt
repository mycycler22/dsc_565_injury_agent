Title: Major and Minor League Baseball Hamstring Injuries: Epidemiologic Findings From the Major League Baseball Injury Surveillance System. | Type: Journal Article, Research Support, Non-U.S. Gov't | PMID: 24727933 | Year: 2014 | Injury query: muscle strain
[RESULTS] In the major leagues, 50 hamstring strains were reported for an injury rate (IR) of 0.7 per 1000 A-Es and averaged 24 days missed.
[RESULTS] In the minor leagues, 218 hamstring strains were reported for an IR of 0.7 per 1000 A-Es and averaged 27 days missed.
[RESULTS] Approximately two-thirds of these injuries in both the major and minor leagues resulted in more than 7 days of time loss.
