Title: Return to Play and Recurrence After Calf Muscle Strain Injuries in Elite Australian Football Players. | Type: Journal Article, Research Support, Non-U.S. Gov't | PMID: 33030961 | Year: 2020 | Injury query: muscle strain
[RESULTS] Irrespective of the anatomical location, players with CMSI with severe aponeurotic disruption (AD) took longer to RTP than players with CMSI with no AD: 31.3 ± 12.6 days vs 19.4 ± 10.8 days (mean ± SD; <i>P</i> = .003).
