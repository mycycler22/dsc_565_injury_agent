Title: Primary repair with semitendinosus tendon augmentation of acute anterior cruciate ligament injuries. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 2405722 | Year: 1990 | Injury query: patellofemoral pain
[UNLABELED] All patients underwent 6 weeks of postoperative immobilization followed by a graduated rehabilitation regimen lasting a mean 7.2 months.
[UNLABELED] This study suggests that primary repair with semitendinosus tendon augmentation of acute ACL injuries with a graduated rehabilitation regimen provides good subjective, and excellent functional and objective, results in active patients that were followed for more than 3 years.
