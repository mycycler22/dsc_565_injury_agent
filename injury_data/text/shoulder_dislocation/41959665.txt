[RESULTS] The process produced aligned rehabilitation protocols comprising five sequential phases: (1) Acute protection and education (0-3 weeks), (2) Movement and early strengthening (3-6 weeks), (3) Progressive strengthening (6-12 weeks), and (4) Return to sport (>12 weeks) and (5) high-level function.
