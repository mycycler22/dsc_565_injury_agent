Title: Six-week physical rehabilitation protocol for anterior shoulder dislocation in athletes. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 28702449 | Year: 2017 | Injury query: shoulder dislocation
[UNLABELED] Participants began a physical rehabilitation program 2 weeks after the shoulder dislocation, which was confirmed by a referring physician.
[UNLABELED] The rehabilitation program was 6 weeks in duration and required the participants to engage in progressive resistive loads/duration using elastic bands and weights 5 days per week.
