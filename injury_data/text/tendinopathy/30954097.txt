Title: Biceps Tenodesis Is a Viable Option for Management of Proximal Biceps Injuries in Patients Less Than 25 Years of Age. | Type: Journal Article, Research Support, Non-U.S. Gov't | PMID: 30954097 | Year: 2019 | Injury query: tendinopathy
[CONCLUSIONS] Biceps tenodesis in patients younger than 25 years yields satisfactory outcomes, with two-thirds of patients returning to sport and a low revision rate.
