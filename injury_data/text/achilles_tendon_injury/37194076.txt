Title: Pro-inflammatory activity of long noncoding RNA FOXD2-AS1 in Achilles tendinopathy. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 37194076 | Year: 2023 | Injury query: Achilles tendon injury
[UNLABELED] Rats were euthanized after 3 weeks, and the effect of FOXD2-AS1, miR-21-3p, or PTEN on Achilles tendon healing was analyzed by histological observation, biomechanical test, and examinations of inflammatory factors and tendon markers.
