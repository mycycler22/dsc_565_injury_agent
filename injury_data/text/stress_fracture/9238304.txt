Title: Epidemiology and site specificity of stress fractures. | Type: Journal Article, Review | PMID: 9238304 | Year: 1997 | Injury query: stress fracture
[UNLABELED] Generally, a period of 6 to 8 weeks is needed for healing; however, stress fractures at certain sites, such as the navicular and anterior tibial cortex, are often associated with protracted recovery and, in some cases, termination of sporting pursuits.
