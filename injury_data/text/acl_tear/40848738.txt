Title: Twelve-month magnetic resonance imaging after anterior cruciate ligament reconstruction can identify risk factors for subsequent graft rupture and used to guide the return to sport. Results from a high-volume institution. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 40848738 | Year: 2025 | Injury query: ACL tear
[CONCLUSION] Higher graft signal on MRI at 12 months, indicating poorer graft healing, is associated with early anterior cruciate ligament retear.
