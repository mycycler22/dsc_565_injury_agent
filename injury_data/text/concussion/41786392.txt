Title: Diagnosis and Management of Concussion. | Type: Journal Article, Review | PMID: 41786392 | Year: 2026 | Injury query: concussion
[UNLABELED] Concussion occurs when a traumatic force of impact to the body or to the head results in a neurologic syndrome characterized by the acute onset of physical, cognitive, emotional, and sleep disruption that typically resolves over 2 weeks.
