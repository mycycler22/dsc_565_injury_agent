Title: Prognostic Factors in Pediatric Sport-Related Concussion. | Type: Journal Article, Review | PMID: 30397831 | Year: 2018 | Injury query: concussion
[RECENT FINDINGS] While the majority of youth athletes recover within 2 to 4 weeks, approximately 10% of athletes experience a protracted recovery with symptoms lasting months, impacting social, scholastic, and sporting activities.
