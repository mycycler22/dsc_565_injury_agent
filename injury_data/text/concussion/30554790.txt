Title: Concussion Incidence, Duration, and Return to School and Sport in 5- to 14-Year-Old American Football Athletes. | Type: Journal Article, Research Support, Non-U.S. Gov't | PMID: 30554790 | Year: 2019 | Injury query: concussion
[RESULTS] After a concussion, 50% of athletes returned to school by 3 days, 50% returned to sport by 13 days, and 50% returned to a baseline level of symptoms by 3 weeks.
