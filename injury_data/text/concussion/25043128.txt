Title: Return to play for neurosurgical patients. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 25043128 | Year: 2014 | Injury query: concussion
[RESULTS] There was a significant difference in time to return between spine (88% returned ≤6 months) versus cranial (50% returned >6 months, P<0.001), noninstrumented (55% returned ≤3 months) versus instrumented spine (92% returned >3 months, P=0.001), and adult (78% returned ≤6 months) versus pediatric cases (52% returned >6 months, P=0.021).
