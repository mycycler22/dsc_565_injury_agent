Title: Turf Toe Injuries in the Athlete: an Updated Review of Treatment Options, Rehabilitation Protocols, and Return-to-Play Outcomes. | Type: Journal Article, Review | PMID: 37789169 | Year: 2023 | Injury query: turf toe
[RECENT FINDINGS] Rehabilitation protocols following non-operative management consist of 3 phases lasting up to 10 weeks, whereas protocols following operative management consist of 4 phases lasting up 20 weeks.
[RECENT FINDINGS] For grade I injuries, players may return to play once they experience minimal to no pain with normal weightbearing, traditionally after 3-5 days.
[RECENT FINDINGS] For grade II injuries, or partial tears, players typically lose 2-4 weeks of play and may need additional support with taping when returning to play.
