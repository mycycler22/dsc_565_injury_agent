Title: Return to sports following operatively treated ankle fractures. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 19356351 | Year: 2009 | Injury query: ankle fracture
[RESULTS] Younger age was predictive of return to sports by 3 months (p = 0.02), 6 months (p = 0.02) and 12 months (p = 0.0001).
[RESULTS] Males were more likely to return to sports at 6 (p = 0.001) and 12 months (p = 0.040).
[RESULTS] At 1 year, 88% of recreational athletes had returned to sports, while only 11.6% of competitive athletes had returned to sports (p = 0.043).
[RESULTS] At 12 months, bimalleolar injuries were more likely to return to sports than unimalleolar ankle fractures (p = 0.042).
