Title: Eighty-two per cent of male professional football (soccer) players return to play at the previous level two seasons after Achilles tendon rupture treated with surgical repair. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 31362925 | Year: 2020 | Injury query: Achilles tendon rupture
[RESULTS] 118 athletes (mean age 27.2±7.2 years) were included. 113 (96%) returned to unrestricted practice after a mean of 199±53 days, with faster recovery in players involved in national teams.
[RESULTS] Return to competition was after a mean of 274±114 days.
[RESULTS] Six players (8%) sustained a re-rupture within the first two seasons after return to play; four re-ruptures were in footballers who returned to play <180 days after injury.
