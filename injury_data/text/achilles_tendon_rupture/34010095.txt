Title: Return to play, performance, and value of National Basketball Association players following Achilles tendon rupture. | Type: Journal Article | PMID: 34010095 | Year: 2021 | Injury query: Achilles tendon rupture
[UNLABELED] The return-to-play rate from Achilles tendon ruptures from 1996-1997 to 2016-2017 was 80%, with a mean recovery period of 311.0 ± 100.9 days.
