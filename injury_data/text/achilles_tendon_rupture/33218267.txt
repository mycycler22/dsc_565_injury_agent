Title: Career Outlook and Performance of Professional Athletes After Achilles Tendon Rupture: A Systematic Review. | Type: Journal Article, Systematic Review | PMID: 33218267 | Year: 2021 | Injury query: Achilles tendon rupture
[RESULTS] Athletes were able to return to professional sport participation 76% of the time, with mean time to RTP of 11 months following AT injury.
[CONCLUSION] Of those able to return to compete at a professional level, the mean time to RTP is 11 months-nearly double the estimated 6-month recovery for RTP in the general population.
