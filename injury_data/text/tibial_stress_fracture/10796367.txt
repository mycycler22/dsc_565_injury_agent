Title: Interventions for preventing and treating stress fractures and stress reactions of bone of the lower limbs in young adults. | Type: Journal Article, Systematic Review | PMID: 10796367 | Year: 2000 | Injury query: tibial stress fracture
[MAIN RESULTS] The use of pneumatic braces in the rehabilitation of tibial stress fractures significantly reduces the time to recommencing training (weighted mean difference -42.6 days, 95% confidence interval -55.8 to -29.4 days).
