Title: Accelerating Recovery: A Case Report on Telerehabilitation for a Triathlete's Post-Meniscus Surgery Comeback. | Type: Case Reports, Journal Article | PMID: 39997281 | Year: 2025 | Injury query: meniscus tear
[UNLABELED] The program was structured into three progressive phases over 12 weeks, focusing on restoring range of motion (ROM), muscle strength, and functional stability while gradually reintroducing sports-specific activities. <b>Results:</b> By the end of the rehabilitation, the patient achieved full ROM and muscle strength (scoring 5/5 on the Medical Research Council scale for the vastus medialis), along with a pain-free state in both static and dynamic conditions.
