Title: Low-intensity pulsed ultrasound in the treatment of traumatic hand fracture in an elite athlete. | Type: Case Reports, Journal Article | PMID: 15624571 | Year: 2004 | Injury query: hand fracture
[UNLABELED] A single 20-min daily application of low-intensity pulsed ultrasound (frequency, 1.5 Mhz; intensity, 30 mW/cm2) provided by Exogen's Sonic Accelerated Fracture Healing System was administered for 24 days on end.
[UNLABELED] After 2 months, radiographs demonstrated the complete healing, with no displacement, of the fracture sites.
